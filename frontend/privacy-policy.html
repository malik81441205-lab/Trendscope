🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Introduction

At TrendScope, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and share your personal information when you use our YouTube trends dashboard and analytics platform.

This policy is designed to comply with global data protection standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), and other applicable privacy regulations.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways when you interact with our platform:

1. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you register for a TrendScope account, we collect your name, email address, password, country, and gender.
  • OAuth / Social Logins: If you register or log in using Google, we receive authorization credentials, profile picture, and email from Google's identity services.
  • Feedback & Support: When you submit feedback or contact support, we collect the feedback message, rating, and contact information you choose to provide.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies to store your preferences, keep you logged in, and analyze usage. You can control these via our Cookie Settings.
  • Analytics Data: If you consent, we collect information about your interactions with our site (pages visited, buttons clicked) using tools like Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.
  • Log Data: We automatically record technical information, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and referrer URLs, for security and diagnostics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide our services: Creating and managing your user account, displaying personalized trends, and processing saved keywords/videos.
  • To improve the platform: Designing new features, resolving performance bottlenecks, and optimizing UI/UX.
  • To maintain security: Preventing malicious activity, bot detection (via reCAPTCHA), preventing spam, and protecting user accounts.
  • To communicate with you: Sending critical system notifications, account verification codes, and responses to support or feedback messages.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, subject to strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Analytics Providers: Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel (only loaded after explicit consent).
  • Authentication: Google Identity Services (for sign-in integrations).
  • Security & Spam Prevention: Google reCAPTCHA (used during signup and login verification).
  • Email Services: Third-party mail relays to deliver verification codes and transactional notifications.

Data Security & Retention

We implement robust security measures, including HTTPS encryption, password hashing, and token-based API authentication, to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

We store your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You can delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, or limit the processing of your data, or withdraw consent to non-essential cookies.

To exercise any of your rights or request account deletion, please contact us at the email address listed below.
