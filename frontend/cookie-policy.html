🍪 Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to the owners of the site.

TrendScope uses cookies and similar technologies to ensure our platform functions correctly, to understand how you interact with our analytics dashboard, and to remember your preferences across visits.

How We Use Cookies

We categorize the cookies we use into three groups. Each group serves a specific purpose and you have control over the optional categories.

Cookie Categories

✅ Required Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionalities such as session management, authentication, and security. Without these cookies, the services you have asked for cannot be provided.

  • User authentication and session management
  • Security features (CSRF protection, rate limiting)
  • Cookie consent preferences storage
  • Theme and language preferences (when classified as essential)

📊 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with TrendScope by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This data allows us to improve our platform and provide a better experience.

  • Page view and session tracking
  • User interaction analytics (clicks, scroll depth)
  • Feature usage statistics
  • Performance monitoring and error reporting

⚙️ Preference Cookies (Optional)

Preference cookies enable the website to remember information that changes the way the site behaves or looks, such as your preferred dashboard layout, selected markets, or regional settings.

  • Dashboard layout and view preferences
  • Selected default markets and regions
  • Content filtering preferences
  • Notification settings

Third-Party Services

When you consent to analytics cookies, TrendScope may integrate with the following third-party services. Each service has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics — Website traffic analysis and reporting. Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Clarity — User behavior heatmaps and session recordings. Privacy Policy
  • Meta Pixel — Conversion tracking and audience measurement. Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads — Advertising conversion tracking. Privacy Policy

No third-party analytics or advertising scripts are loaded unless you explicitly consent to analytics cookies. If you reject analytics cookies, none of these services will be activated.

Managing Your Cookies

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using our Cookie Settings panel. You can access it by:

  • Clicking "Manage Cookies" in the website footer
  • Using the cookie settings button that appeared when you first visited

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of TrendScope.

Update Your Preferences

Open the cookie settings panel to review and change your current preferences.

Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access — You can request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification — You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure — You can request the deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Restrict Processing — You can ask us to limit the processing of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability — You can request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object — You can object to the processing of your personal data for certain purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — You can withdraw your consent to cookies at any time via our cookie settings.
